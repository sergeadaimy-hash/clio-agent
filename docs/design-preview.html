CLIO, Frontend Rebuild, Design Preview

Control Room
Calm.

A show-control aesthetic for live event operations. Built for two people: the HOD on a dark festival site at 11pm who needs to file a report in under two minutes, and the admin at a desk who needs the whole event's status at a glance. Every color below is a white-label token, replaceable from the Brand tab.

Captions are first-class since they print under each photo in the deck. Clear confirmation, timestamp receipt, and one obvious way to edit before the deadline.
AGuided steps replace the long formThe current portal is one tall scroll. Three focused steps lower the effort at the end of a 14 hour day and raise completion quality.
BDeadline pressure made visibleA countdown pill on every screen. Late HODs are the number one operational problem this tool solves; the UI should carry that urgency quietly.
COffline-tolerant autosaveDrafts persist in localStorage per department and date. Site wifi is unreliable; losing a typed report is the fastest way to lose adoption.

GEverything is loggedOutbound reminders, delivery ticks from Meta status webhooks, and inbound replies all land in a whatsapp_messages table and render as threads grouped by HOD.
HTest-mode honestyThe amber badge shows you are on the Meta test number with its 5 recipient limit, so nobody wonders why a sixth HOD gets nothing. Swapping to a production number later is only an env change.
IReply from the consoleThe composer sends free-form messages inside Meta's 24 hour customer service window; outside it, CLIO falls back to approved templates automatically.
JHuman takeover per threadBy default the CLIO agent auto-replies to HOD messages with context-aware answers (deadline, submission status). The amber Take over button switches that thread to human mode: the agent goes silent, the composer unlocks, and a Return to agent action hands the thread back. The mode is stored per conversation and visible in the thread list.
